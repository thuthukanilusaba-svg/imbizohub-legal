Common Questions

How does ImbizoHub work?

Most marketplaces make you hunt through listings. ImbizoHub works the other way round: you post what you need, and sellers come to you with their price. Open the app, tap Ask for it, say what you are looking for, and wait for offers. You can compare them, chat with anyone who replies, and ask your questions before you commit to anybody.

You can also browse listings the usual way, or post your own things for sale. Both directions work.

Nobody has responded to my post

Offers are not instant — a seller has to see your post and decide they can supply it. Give it a day. A post with a clear title, a realistic budget and your town filled in gets answered far more often than a vague one. If a few days pass with nothing, edit the post to add detail, or write to us and we will take a look at it.

What does it cost?

Free to list, free to chat, free to do a deal. We only earn a small percentage when you accept a seller's offer on something you asked for, or a transport operator's quote on a trip. There is nothing to pay to browse or to post, and no adverts.

During the launch promotion those fees are free for everyone until 31 January 2027.

I cannot sign in / I forgot my password

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we will email you a reset link. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then write to us from the address on the account.

Hiring transport

Tap Hire Transport, post the trip — where from, where to, what you are moving and when — and registered operators bid for the job. You compare the quotes and pick your driver. Our operators drive vans and small trucks, so anything up to a three-tonne load.

Someone is behaving badly

Every listing and every profile has a Report option. Use it — reports come to us and we act on them. If someone has threatened you or you believe a crime has been committed, contact the police as well; we will cooperate with any investigation.

Deals on ImbizoHub happen face to face. Meet somewhere public and busy, in daylight, and check the item before any money changes hands.

Deleting my account

You can do this yourself, from Profile → Delete my account, at any time. What happens when you delete your account explains exactly what is removed and what we are required to keep.

Reporting a Bug

If the app crashed or a screen will not load, we want to know. Please include:

  • the phone you are using, and whether it is Android or iPhone
  • what you tapped just before it happened
  • a screenshot, if you managed to take one

Send it to support@imbizohub.com. Bug reports from people using the app are the fastest way these get fixed.

Still Stuck?

Write to support@imbizohub.com and tell us what you were trying to do. We would rather hear about a small problem than have you give up on it.
